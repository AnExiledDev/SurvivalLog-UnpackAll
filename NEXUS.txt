Name: Unpack All
Summary: One press unpacks every furniture package in your bag, then picks up the packages on the floor at home and unpacks those too, as far as your home has free package spots. F9 or the inventory's Mods menu.

Description (BBCode):
Unpacking furniture packages one at a time is slow, and lining up too many fills the queue with unpacks that fail. Unpack All unpacks everything in your bag and on the floor at home in one press, and never lines up more than your home has room for.

[size=5]Main features[/size]
[list]
[*][b]Bag first, then the floor[/b]: packages on the floor at home are picked up and unpacked when spots are left.[/*]
[*][b]Never more than fits[/b]: only as many unpacks as your home has free package spots. The rest stay where they are.[/*]
[*][b]Game's own Use action[/b]: the same action as the inventory's Use button, with the game's checks and timings.[/*]
[*][b]Your queued actions stay[/b]: unpacks join the end of the queue, so your own actions run first.[/*]
[*][b]Clear messages[/b]: says how many are unpacking, how many were picked up, and why any were left.[/*]
[*][b]Mods menu[/b]: a Mods button on the inventory toolbar with an Unpack all entry, shared with other mods that use it.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5]Hotkeys[/size]
[list]
[*][b]F9[/b]: unpack all (in a loaded game, past the prologue).[/*]
[/list]
F9 can be changed or turned off in the config. Unpack all in the Mods menu works either way.

The mod adds no data of its own to your save, but the unpacks are real: packages come off the floor into your bag and become furniture. You can remove it at any time; packages and furniture stay as they are.

[size=5]Known issues[/size]
[list]
[*]Back up your save before trying it for the first time.[/*]
[*]Packages beyond your free package spots stay where they are. Place some furniture, then press again.[/*]
[*]Floor packages are only picked up while you are at home. Packages inside storages are not touched.[/*]
[*]The game picks up a floor stack whole, so some packages can end up waiting in your bag for the next press.[/*]
[*]The game's own refusal reasons can show in Chinese.[/*]
[/list]
If you run into anything strange, please tell me (see Feedback below).
